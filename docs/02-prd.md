## Purpose

This document will define, in concrete and testable terms:

- Who the user is (Sid, as both founder and sole operator of V1)
- What each milestone must deliver to be considered "stable" (see CONTRIBUTING.md done-criteria standard)
- Functional requirements per engine (Research, Intelligence, Production, Publishing, Analytics, CEO AI)
- Non-functional requirements (cost ceiling per video, approval time budget, uptime expectations)
- Out-of-scope items for V1 (anything in `BACKLOG.md`)

## Why It's Empty Right Now

Per the project rule established June 2026: nothing gets built ahead of its milestone. The PRD is an M1 deliverable, written once M0 (Technology Validation) confirms what's actually feasible — writing requirements before knowing your constraints produces a fictional spec.
